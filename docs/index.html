Simple payback assumes unchanged annual income. It ignores lease expiry, resale, financing, inflation and future changes. A lease can expire before this period. A nonpositive income has no positive payback period.

What this calculation includes

Acquisition price plus additional purchase costs form the yield denominator. Management and reserve are fractions of gross rent. Fixed costs are deducted once per year. The selected tax scenario uses either gross rent or positive operating profit. There are no country presets or live forecasts.

This tool does not determine legal ownership, current availability, tax residency, lawful deductions or an investment recommendation. Validate inputs for your transaction. No data is submitted, stored or tracked by this calculator.
